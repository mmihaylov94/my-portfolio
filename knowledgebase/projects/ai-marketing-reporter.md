## Summary

AI Marketing Reporter is a weekly automated reporting agent that pulls advertising and analytics data, generates performance insights, and outputs client-ready reporting content.

## Problem Solved

Manual weekly reporting across ad/analytics platforms is time-consuming and inconsistent.

## Solution

- Aggregates data from Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Google Analytics.
- Produces recommendations and summary output for clients.
- Generates a report and ready-to-send email-style output.

## Technologies

- n8n
- JavaScript
- REST API

## Outcome

Automates recurring reporting and generates client-ready recommendations.
