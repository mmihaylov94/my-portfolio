## Summary

PawCircle is a niche social platform for dog owners with profile-driven sharing and community interaction features.

## Problem Solved

General social platforms are broad; niche communities benefit from focused social features tailored to specific interests.

## Solution

- Owner and dog profiles.
- Photo posts, likes, comments, and follow-based feeds.
- Private messaging.
- Baseline moderation features.

## Technologies

- Node
- JavaScript
- Vue
- PostgreSQL
- Tailwind CSS

## Outcome

Supports focused community engagement around pet ownership.
