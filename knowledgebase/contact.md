## Summary

Email is the primary and fastest way to reach Mihail. Two addresses are in use, each for a different purpose. The website also offers a CV download and a contact form for people who prefer one.

## Which email address should someone use?

- **Hiring, recruitment, and anyone working from his CV**: `m.mihaylov94@gmail.com`. This is the address printed on the CV.
- **Project inquiries and general questions through the website**: `mihaylov.dev@gmail.com`. This is the address published on `mihaylov.io`.

Both reach Mihail. Neither is a fallback for the other, so quote the one that matches the reason for writing.

## How else can someone get in touch?

- Main website: `https://mihaylov.io`
- Contact section: `/?section=contact` on the main website, which offers a direct email link above the form.
- CV download: `https://mihaylov.io/Mihail_Mihaylov_CV.pdf`, linked from both the hero and the contact section.
- LinkedIn: `https://www.linkedin.com/in/mihail-m-mihaylov`
- GitHub: `https://github.com/mmihaylov94`

The contact form asks for a reason: project inquiry, collaboration, job opportunity, consultation, or other.

## What is the best channel for a given need?

- **Hiring and job opportunities**: Email `m.mihaylov94@gmail.com`, or LinkedIn.
- **Forwarding his details to a colleague**: Send the CV download link.
- **Project inquiries**: Email `mihaylov.dev@gmail.com`, or the contact form.
- **Collaboration opportunities**: Email or LinkedIn.
- **General questions**: Email is the most direct channel.
