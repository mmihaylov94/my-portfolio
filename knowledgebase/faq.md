## What is Mihail's job title?

Solutions Architect. He is a Solutions Architect at Businessmap, and was previously a Technical Solution Architect at Deloitte LLP UK. Earlier labels such as "Software Developer" or "Full-Stack Developer & Automation Engineer" are out of date.

## What does Mihail specialise in?

Automation, API-led integrations, enterprise RPA, and production AI systems, backed by a strong full-stack engineering background.

## What projects are on the portfolio?

- **Glotsmith**, a commercial language-learning SaaS he designed, built, deployed, and operates alone, at `https://glotsmith.com`. Its case study is at `https://mihaylov.io/case-studies/glotsmith`.
- **The AI assistant on this site**, a retrieval-augmented chat assistant built with n8n, pgvector, and Nuxt.
- **n8n Pro Automation Framework**, a productised automation framework with idempotency, observability, and contract-defined sub-workflows.
- **AI Marketing Reporter**, an automated weekly reporting pipeline producing LLM-written performance reports.
- **Threadline**, a moderated community forum built with CodeIgniter 4 and PostgreSQL, at `https://threadline.mihaylov.io`.

## Which of Mihail's projects are open source?

Threadline and the n8n Pro Automation Framework have public repositories under `https://github.com/mmihaylov94`. Glotsmith is a commercial product and its source is private.

## Which project best shows what Mihail can do?

Glotsmith. It is the only one that covers the whole span: product design, a demanding front end, a layered API, AWS infrastructure, CI/CD with rehearsed disaster recovery, subscription billing, and regulatory compliance implemented in code, all built and operated by one person.

## What is his educational background?

An MSc in Advanced Computer Science, awarded with Distinction, and a BEng (Hons) in Computer and Electronic Systems, both at the University of Strathclyde, Glasgow.

## What certifications does Mihail hold?

Certified Advanced RPA Developer v1.0 (UiPath) and Certified Advanced RPA Professional A360 (Automation Anywhere).

## What technical areas is he strongest in?

Solution architecture, automation and integration design, production AI systems, and end-to-end delivery with production ownership.
