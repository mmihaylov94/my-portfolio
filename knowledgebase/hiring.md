## Summary

Mihail Mihaylov is open to new roles as of August 2026. He is a Solutions Architect based in Sofia, Bulgaria, working remotely for European and US companies.

## What roles is Mihail looking for?

Mihail is open to Solutions Architect, Solutions Engineer, AI Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Automation Engineer, and RPA Developer roles. He is also open to intelligent automation and agentic automation positions.

He is most interested in work that combines process understanding, automation architecture, API integration, and hands-on engineering. Roles that are purely one of those are a weaker fit than roles that span several.

## Is Mihail available for remote work?

Yes. Mihail works remotely from Sofia, Bulgaria, and is available across European business hours and US-overlap hours. He is not currently seeking on-site or relocation roles.

## Can a company hire someone based in Bulgaria?

Yes, and it is straightforward. Bulgaria joined the eurozone in January 2026, so payroll runs in euros and there is no currency conversion risk for a European employer.

Employer of Record providers with full Bulgaria coverage include Deel, Remote.com, G-P (Globalization Partners), Safeguard Global, and WorkMotion. Providers with limited Bulgaria coverage include Oyster HR, Multiplier, and Papaya Global, so a company using one of those should check before assuming.

Mihail can also work as an independent contractor and invoice directly.

## What languages does Mihail speak?

Bulgarian is his native language. English is fluent. He lived in the United Kingdom for ten years, from September 2013 until June 2023, studying at the University of Strathclyde and then working in Glasgow.

Third, he has designed, built, deployed, and operated a production SaaS entirely alone, including the infrastructure, billing, security, and legal obligations. That breadth is unusual.
